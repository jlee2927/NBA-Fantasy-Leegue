Nothing was lost on your side — the server simply does not have this one any more.

This usually means one of:

  • the app was updated or restarted while the draft was open
  • it was left open for more than half a day
  • it was started in a different browser, or a private window
  • the projections were refreshed, so the picks could no longer be matched to the right players

A draft is kept in your own browser so it survives the app restarting. It cannot survive the projections themselves changing underneath it, because the picks would then point at the wrong players — better to say so than to hand you somebody else's team.

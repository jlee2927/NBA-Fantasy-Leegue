ESPN publishes only G, F and C, so the derived split can shuffle a player inside his group but never out of it — Wembanyama is filed under F and cannot reach a centre slot however he plays. This list finds those cases by scoring what a player actually does and asking whether another group fits him better.

It decides nothing. Eligibility is set by whichever platform your league runs on, so check these against Yahoo and write the answers into {{ overrides }}. A high score is frontcourt, a low one is backcourt, and gap is how much better the suggested group fits.

Expect false positives at the top. Jokić is flagged because he passes like a guard, not because his position is wrong — the list finds unusual players, and a miscategorised one is only the most common reason to be unusual.
